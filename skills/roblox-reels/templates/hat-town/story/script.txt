In this town, every lie you tell makes your hat grow one block taller.
One tiny lie, one block.
The baker's hat has six blocks. The mayor's has nineteen.
The only thing taller is the clock tower.
People line up at the barber just to saw their hats down.
Everyone has a tall hat. Except me.
My hat has never grown. Not once.
My best friend Pip said that's impossible.
So he tested me. "Do you like my haircut?"
It was terrible. "I love it," I said.
Nothing.
Then the mayor asked, "Did you vote for me?"
"Of course," I said.
Still nothing. The whole square went quiet.
Then Pip looked up. Way up. At the clock tower.
The one that has always stood right above my head.
It was never a clock tower.
So, how many lies have I told you today?
